## Background

When a deaf sports club runs a league, sign-ups, fixtures and results usually live in spreadsheets and message threads. That puts a lot on the volunteer keeping track, and it is hard for players to see when they play and against whom.

## Brief

An app where players sign themselves up, see the next match day and follow the standings, and where the club is spared the manual puzzle of swaps and substitutes.

## Approach

A SvelteKit app on Cloudflare, built for multiple clubs from the start. Players sign up with their level, choice of division (league or social padel) and a partner, or ask to be matched with one, with capacity limits and a waiting list. The app shows fixtures per court, standings per division, and lets players volunteer as substitutes. Member details are visible only to the club's admin. A demo with made-up players shows the whole flow.

## Outcome

In use at Døvania Padel for the Tuesday league at Kløvermarken, with sign-ups open for the next season. More clubs are on the way.
