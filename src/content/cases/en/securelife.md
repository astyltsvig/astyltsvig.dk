## Background

In insurance and pensions, data is in constant motion, and the demands on security, traceability, and correct handling are uncompromising. Many stakeholders work in the same material from different angles — and nothing may be lost or changed without a trace.

That calls for a platform that is strict about access and business rules, yet flexible enough to follow how the work is actually done.

## The platform

The platform administers insurance policies and pension schemes across hundreds of companies and thousands of employees: who is covered by what, which documents belong to which scheme, and what changed when.

The foundation is a fine-grained access system, reusable data models across providers, and automated workflows around changes that require follow-up. Two brands run on the same platform — one codebase, consistent quality, and every improvement counting twice.

## My role

Architecture, development, and operations across the platform's entire lifetime — from the first foundation in 2021, through today's daily production, to the ongoing work on the next generation.

## Outcome

In stable daily production since 2022, carrying business-critical processes for two brands. Developed continuously — in step with the business, not behind it.
