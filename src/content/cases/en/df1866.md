## Background

Døveforeningen af 1866 is one of Denmark's oldest Deaf associations and also operates an event venue for hire. The website was dated, and DNS sat with a provider that had become a bottleneck for everything the association wanted to change.

## Brief

A proper digital presence, a DNS setup the association controls itself — and a migration where Google Workspace email could not be down for even a minute.

## Approach

A new Astro site on Cloudflare Pages. DNS migrated from Gigahost to Cloudflare with precise handling of MX records, so mail routing ran uninterrupted through the entire cutover. On top: a professional PDF price list for the venue rental, giving the association one document to send to prospective renters.

## Outcome

Live at df1866.dk with running costs close to zero — Cloudflare Pages is free at this scale. No lost mail, no vendor lock-in, and a foundation that can be built on.
