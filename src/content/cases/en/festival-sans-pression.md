## Background

Festival Sans Pression is a two-day festival in Toulouse on 3 and 4 October 2026, organised by Association Parenthèse for the FLINTA community (femmes, lesbiennes, intersexes, non binaires, trans, agenres). The festival offers workshops, conversations, and an alcohol-free setting: "sans codes, sans masques, sans pression".

## Brief

Carry the poster's illustrated universe (by the artist BM) all the way to the web. Programme, tickets, and practical information had to be easy to find, and accessibility had to be considered from the start.

## Approach

A static Astro site on Cloudflare Pages. The pop-art palette of pastel violet, pink, and turquoise carries the whole site, set with generous whitespace and large typographic statements drawn from the poster's universe. Three display modes (default, dark, and high contrast) keep the site usable for visitors with different visual needs, without losing the identity.

## Outcome

The site in the portfolio I'm proudest of aesthetically: an artist-led identity carried all the way through, on a site that is also accessible and simple to run.
