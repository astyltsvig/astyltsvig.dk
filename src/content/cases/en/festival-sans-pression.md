## Background

Festival Sans Pression is a two-day festival in Toulouse on 3–4 October 2026, organised by Association Parenthèse for the FLINTA community (femmes, lesbiennes, intersexes, non binaires, trans, agenres). Workshops, conversations, and an alcohol-free setting — "sans codes, sans masques, sans pression".

## Brief

Carry the poster's illustrated universe (by the artist BM) all the way to the web. Programme, tickets, and practical information had to be razor-sharp — and accessibility had to be a baseline, not an option.

## Approach

A static Astro site on Cloudflare Pages. The pop-art palette — pastel violet, pink, and turquoise — carries the whole site, set with generous whitespace and large typographic statements drawn from the poster's universe. Three display modes — default, dark, and high contrast — keep the site usable for visitors with different visual needs without surrendering the identity.

## Outcome

The site in the portfolio I'm proudest of aesthetically: an artist-led identity carried through without sacrificing accessibility or a simple, dependable operating model.
