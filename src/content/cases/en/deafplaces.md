## Background

Can I order here? Will the guide understand me? Is there a deaf club in town? Ordinary reviews rarely say whether the staff sign. Deaf people know, but that knowledge is scattered across Facebook groups and conversations.

## Brief

One map for the whole world, built by deaf people, where deaf travellers can find places to use sign language, and where places can put themselves on the map for free.

## Approach

A Next.js app on Cloudflare with an interactive globe, search by city and category, and "places near me". Every place gets a badge so you know what to expect: deaf-owned, deaf staff or sign-friendly. Reviews can be written as text or recorded as video in your own sign language, and volunteer country admins check places in their own country. No tracking cookies and no selling of data.

## Outcome

Live at deafplaces.app with more than 540 places in 91 countries and 82 sign languages, with new places added every day. The map is free for travellers, and the plan is to open-source the code so it can live on in the deaf community.
