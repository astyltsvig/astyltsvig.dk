## Background

Care Pension & Forsikring advises companies and their employees on pensions and insurance. The advisory work is built on an Excel-based tool with PensionsBroker — professionally strong, but a world of its own, separate from what customers themselves can see and access.

## Brief

Give customers a portal where their schemes and material live in one place — and build the bridge between the portal and the advisory tool, so the two worlds work on the same data instead of separate copies.

## Approach

A customer portal developed from scratch for Care Pension & Forsikring's needs, plus integration tools connecting the portal to the Excel-based advisory tool. The adviser's work and the customer's view stay in sync — no duplicated effort, no manual go-betweens.

## Outcome

An ongoing collaboration where the advisory business and the technology evolve together: new needs in the advisory work become new integrations and features in the portal.
