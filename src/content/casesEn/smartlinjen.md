## Background

The telephone is still a wall for deaf and hard-of-hearing people: jobs, healthcare, banking, and everyday life run on phone calls, while texts get ignored and interpreter bookings take days. Around 800,000 Danes have reduced hearing — and today no solution makes Danish phone calls accessible.

Smartlinjen is an internal project at AStyltsvig Consulting, built to change that.

## The idea

A phone that speaks text: everything the caller says appears as text in real time — and the user's written replies are read aloud in a natural Danish voice the user has chosen. A personal intro tells the caller what is happening, so the conversation feels normal at both ends.

## The solution

Own technology built for Danish from the first line: live transcription with under 2 seconds of latency, natural Danish speech synthesis with voice selection, and AI quick replies that continuously suggest three short answers so the conversation keeps its rhythm. On top of that: push notifications on iPhone and Android, a transcribed voicemail, and searchable history.

Accessibility and privacy are foundational: every state is communicated with colour, icon, and text at once (WCAG AA), no human interpreters listen in, raw audio is never stored, and transcripts live in the EU with one-click deletion.

## Status

The prototype works today and completes real calls: inbound and outbound calls with live Danish transcription, speech synthesis, AI quick replies, a personal dictionary, and a GDPR-ready data model. Next up: using your own number via call forwarding and pilot runs with the first daily users — with a vision of an accessible phone number for every deaf and hard-of-hearing person in Denmark.
