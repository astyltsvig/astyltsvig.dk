## Background

Shows land scattered across streaming services and broadcast schedules, and it's easy to lose track of when the next episode arrives — and how far you've come yourself. Shows Calendar is an internal project at AStyltsvig Consulting: one delightful, free place to keep track of it all.

## The idea

Never miss an episode again: follow your shows, see every upcoming episode in one combined monthly calendar in your own timezone, and mark what you've watched — individually, whole seasons, or catch up on everything at once.

## The solution

A Next.js app on Cloudflare Pages where air dates and episodes are fetched live from TVmaze — no manual entry. Every user gets a personal iCal feed, so new episodes automatically appear in Google or Apple Calendar. Accounts are created with email or Google in ten seconds, and the whole interface is available in both Danish and English.

## Outcome

A free product in daily production that solves a very concrete everyday problem: the calendar keeps itself up to date, progress follows along — and the next episode is never a surprise again.
